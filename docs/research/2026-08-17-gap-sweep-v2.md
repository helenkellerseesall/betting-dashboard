## 1. NEW since 7/29 (external)

**1.1 🔴 THE 90% GAMBLING-LOSS CAP IS LIVE FOR TAX YEAR 2026 — REAL, and structurally hostile to exactly our style of betting.**
Effective for tax years beginning after 2025-12-31, IRC §165(d) now caps deductible gambling losses at **90% of losses** — a break-even year produces taxable "phantom income" ([National Tax Tools §165(d) guide](https://nationaltaxtools.com/guides/gambling-loss-deduction/), [MyCPAPro/OBBBA](https://mycpapro.com/blog/obbba-gambling-loss-deduction), [Morkel](https://morkelfinancial.com/blog/gambling-loss-deduction-90-percent-limit-2026) [PRAC, multi-source concordant]). Win $10k / lose $10k ⇒ $1k taxable. The Fair Bet Act (Titus) would repeal it but is unpassed — **file assuming the cap** [PRAC]. Also NEW: **W-2G threshold rose $600 → $2,000**, and the IRS expects a **session-level contemporaneous log** (date, location, wager type, net per session) — not a per-bet dump.
**Why this is a top-3 gap for us specifically:** high-turnover, high-gross-churn styles (longshot ladders, parlay volume, promo churn) maximize *gross* winnings and *gross* losses relative to net — the exact shape the 90% cap punishes. At today's $1–2 stakes the dollar impact is trivial; **at any scaled version of the Lab it is a real drag that must be modeled before scaling, not after.** Deduction only helps if itemizing at all — for many filers the standard deduction makes losses non-deductible entirely, worsening it.
**What we must have (none exists today [REPO: grep — no tax/session artifacts]):** the ledger already stores per-bet stake/odds/result/timestamps — a **session-level rollup export** (ET day = session, per book) is a small report-layer build, not new data. → ASK candidate.

**1.2 Prediction markets have overtaken sportsbook volume — SPECULATIVE for us, but the trend line moved fast.** ~$24B/month global prediction-market trading (April 2026) vs ~$14B/month legal US sportsbook handle (2025) ([GamingToday](https://www.gamingtoday.com/prediction-markets/sports/), [CBS state tracker](https://www.cbssports.com/prediction/news/prediction-market-legal-states/) [PRAC]). Legal patchwork is churning (NV most restrictive; MA/MI/WA limited under court orders; a dozen+ active suits; CFTC pushing back on the "it's just betting" argument; NBA formally asked CFTC for limits on its markets [PRAC: [Covers, Aug 2026](https://www.covers.com/industry/cftc-duck-sports-betting-prediction-markets-new-rules-warning-august-2026)]). **Unchanged verdict from 07-05: execution venue, not modeling surface — and STILL blocked on the same one-line operator answer (which venues are legal in your state?), now 6 weeks unanswered.**

**1.3 State-level deposit/access rules are now a live variable — NEW.** Colorado's SB 26-131 (effective 2026-08-12) caps online sportsbook customers at six deposits per 24h gaming day and bans credit-card funding [PRAC: [Community News](https://communitynews.org/gambling-news/colorado-sports-betting-deposit-limits-credit-card-ban/)]. Not necessarily our state — the point is that **operational rules now change under us mid-season**; the sweep should re-check the operator's state each cycle.

**1.4 Bankroll practice — consensus reaffirmed, and it indicts our current practice.** Flat betting until you have two full seasons of tracked results; then ¼–½ Kelly, never full (full Kelly showed 35%+ drawdowns); 1–2% units; hard cap ~5% of bankroll regardless of edge ([XCLSV 2026 guide](https://xclsvmedia.com/sports-betting-bankroll-management-in-2026-the-complete-guide-to-protecting-your-profits/), [Sportstrade fractional-Kelly](https://www.sportstrade.io/blog-detail/141/the-fractional-kelly-bankroll-management-system.html), [arXiv 2107.08827 experimental review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.08827) [PRAC/AUTH]). Flat is CORRECT for our phase — but "flat" must be a written number, not a vibe (§3.1).
